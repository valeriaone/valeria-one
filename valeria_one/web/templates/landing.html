What a business gets

One inboxWhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, SMS, email, website — one list, the country’s rules enforced.
One-click Meta connectEmbedded Signup, Login for Business, Lead Ads, comment → private message.
AI with the business’s own keyAnswers only from its prices and hours, in the customer’s language; hands off when unsure.
BookingInside the chat (tap-to-pick on WhatsApp) and on a page; Google Calendar both ways.
Quotes & e-signatureThe country’s tax, signed on the phone, SHA-256 fingerprint.
PaymentsThe business’s own Stripe, Mercado Pago (Pix), PayMongo (GCash), Paystack, Razorpay (UPI).
Follow-ups & campaignsReminders, thank-yous, campaigns — consent, quiet hours and the 24-hour window built in.
A websiteFor businesses that only have a Facebook page; contact form straight into the inbox.
